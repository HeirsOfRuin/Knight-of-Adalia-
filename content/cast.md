# Cast Sheet

The working reference for every recurring character: how they look and talk, what they want, what they fear, what they hide, and where they are going. Keep scenes consistent with it, and update it when a scene settles something new. Spoilers are expected; this is for writers, not players.

**Voice rules for all cast**
- Nobody explains their own feelings in a sentence that a modern therapist would say.
- People talk around things, joke about things, or say the practical thing instead.
- Each recurring character has at least one verbal habit and one physical habit, used sparingly: once a scene, not once a line.

## Family (one set per background)

### Reeve's son, Ashby
- **Piers the reeve.**
  - **Look and voice:** Forty, careful, precise, proud of his hand. Speaks in short, measured sentences. Nods when he is thinking, and keeps nodding.
  - **What drives him:** Wants to stay reeve and to see his son rise further than any Ashby man has. Afraid of being found out, if he skims, or of being blamed, if he does not.
  - **Secret:** Either he has skimmed for years (`father_skims`) or he knows the steward has.
  - **Arc:** Ch1 crisis (exposed, shielded or confessed). Ch3: what he thinks of his son as a lord who has his own reeve.
- **Edith, his mother.** (named in the home scene, "The Dairy")
  - **Look and voice:** Practical, devout, quietly braver than her husband. Speaks to her son in the dairy, where Piers cannot hear.
  - **History:** Lost her first son at four, to a winter fever. His blue tunic waits in the chest.
- **Cis and Avice, his younger sisters.**
  - Cis is sharp and Avice is shy.
  - Their dowries will be his problem in Ch3.
- **Master Gervase Holt, the steward.**
  - **Look and voice:** Thin, coughing, a bald fur collar. Patient, clever, petty. Says "We shall see." Looks at roofs when he lies.
  - **What drives him:** Steals because his salary has not risen in twenty years and Sir Hamon never checks. Wants a comfortable old age. Afraid of the boy who can count.
- **Father Anselm, priest of Ashby.**
  - **Look and voice:** A big, soft farmer's son with bad Latin and good weather sense. Keeps bees. Hums psalms.
  - **Role:** Holds the tally copy in his psalter. Will be the protagonist's link home.
- **Wat and Mag Coker.**
  - **Wat:** Big and bitter. His family lost their holding on Piers's word. Throws stones, then later stands in the levy.
  - **Mag:** Sharp-tongued, watchful, clever, poor. She is the one who decides things in that family.

### Burgess's son, Wendham
- **Ralf Mercer.**
  - **Look and voice:** Wool merchant on the council. A tidy man who writes everything down and speaks to his son in the tone he uses for customers.
  - **What drives him:** Ashamed of his trade in front of knights, and proud of it everywhere else. Wants a son in the gentry. Afraid of the Lanzi debt.
- **Margery Mercer, his mother.**
  - A poor knight's daughter from Hollesby (Sir Roger, who fought at Harrow Ford), married to wool for money. Keeps her father's sword over the hearth and his spurs in a box; gives the spurs to her son when he leaves if he sat with her.
- **Thomas Penny, the clerk.** Old, slow, jealous of a boy who does sums faster.
- **Master Osbert Fuller, guild warden.**
  - **Look and voice:** Fat and shrewd. His daughter does his sums. Says "Well now" before every bargain.
- **Cecily Fuller.**
  - **Look and voice:** Neat, quick, unsentimental, ink-stained. Asks the questions nobody else asks: how much, and when.
  - **What drives her:** Wants a husband who is going somewhere and will let her do the books. Afraid of marrying a fool.
- **Bertuccio Lanzi, Sarenzan factor.**
  - **Look and voice:** Courteous, amused. Speaks Adalian with care and slightly too correctly.
  - **Role:** Likes the boy because the boy reads contracts.
- **Jocelin Tanner.**
  - **Look and voice:** Clever and resentful. Keeps lists of who wronged him.
- **Annot Dyer.**
  - **Look and voice:** Blue hands, a loud laugh. Unimpressed by anyone.

### Veteran archer's son
- **Hugh Fletcher.**
  - **Look and voice:** Fifty, a ruined knee, still the best shot in the parish. Laconic. Drinks to forget Carn Dubh, and to remember it.
  - **What drives him:** Wants his son to have what he did not: a name.
- **Will Cobb, serjeant.**
  - **Look and voice:** A voice like a millrace, hands like roots. Grey beard, new shirts. Tells war stories for ale. Loves Hugh Fletcher like a brother and owes him something unspecified.
  - **Arc:** Will be in the Ch2 army. May die there.
- **Alison, his mother, and Kit, his sister.** Alison spins for a Wendham clothier and never stops; asks him to come home from any war "all of you". Kit is three years younger and follows him everywhere.
- **Home:** Hollin, a dozen cottages on the edge of the Ravell chase.
- **Gib Shawe.**
  - **Look and voice:** The bailiff's nephew. Cannot draw a war bow and is terrified someone will find out.
- **Mariot Wood.**
  - **Look and voice:** The forester's daughter. Quiet, exact, knows every path in the chase.
  - **What drives her:** Her father's place depends on his health.

### Household servant's son, Ravell Hall
- **Agnes, his mother, tirewoman to Lady Ravell.**
  - **Look and voice:** Proud, anxious, devoted to the family she serves. Straightens collars. Never raises her voice.
- **Nell, a laundry girl.**
  - **Look and voice:** Laughs at everyone, including the family. Brave in small ways.
- **Master Ranulf, the marshal.** See Ravell Hall.

## Ravell Hall
- **Lord Thurstan Ravell.**
  - **Look and voice:** Fifty, heavy, intelligent, cold. Speaks little and remembers everything.
  - **What drives him:** Wants Ravell to outlast him and is willing to sell anything else to make sure it does. Afraid of Carrow and of the King's war bankrupting the shire.
- **Hob Crane, head groom.** See below. Davy Ludd's grandfather.
- **Lady Eleanor Ravell.**
  - **Look and voice:** Elegant, devout, hard. Hears through walls. Burns juniper against everything.
  - **Secret:** The heir's real father is not her husband. This is the household secret the servant's son knows.
- **Aymer Ravell, the second son.**
  - **Look and voice:** Fair, quick, bored, generous with other people's money. Gambles.
  - **What drives him:** Wants to matter in a house where his elder brother inherits everything.
  - **Arc:** Ch4 court ally or rival.
- **Lady Maud de Lisle, the ward.**
  - **Look and voice:** Fifteen at Ch1, heiress of Lisle. Composed in public, fierce in private. Speaks quickly and low.
  - **What drives her:** Refuses to be sold to Carrow's son.
- **Isabel Sayer, waiting-woman.**
  - **Look and voice:** A narrow clever face, ink on her forefinger. Funny, and the funnier when unhappy.
  - **Situation:** The fourth of seven daughters. Her father will marry her to the first knight who asks.
- **Sir Bertram Oakes, master-at-arms.**
  - **Look and voice:** Sixty, missing two fingers. Says "You're dead" more often than anything else. Fair to everyone and kind to no one, on principle.
- **Master Ranulf, the marshal.**
  - **Look and voice:** Narrow, grey-whiskered, a leather apron. Has run the stables for thirty years. Kicks the straw to see what is underneath. Approval is a nod.
- **Father Benet, chaplain and clerk.**
  - **Look and voice:** Worldly, witty. Knows where everything went. Owes and is owed small favours all over the shire.
- **Giles Marrick, squire.**
  - **Look and voice:** Nineteen, good teeth, a better sword. Smiles at the side of your head.
  - **What drives him:** A second son with nothing but his father's name and his own contempt. Afraid that churls will rise and men like him will not.
  - **Arc:** Knighted at Christmas in Ch1. In the Ch2 army.
- **Hob Crane, head groom.**
  - **Look and voice:** Bent, old, profane, fair.

## The masters
- **Sir Hamon Darrell of Ashby.**
  - **Wife:** Alice, dead eleven years in childbed. She kept the accounts; Ashby was solvent while she lived. He still talks to her at night.
  - **Look and voice:** Fifty-six, big, going soft. A red face, white hair, an uncalculated smile. Loves his hounds. Says "Well, well." Feeds people.
  - **What drives him:** Wants to be thought a good lord. Has never once checked an account.
  - **Arc:** Ch1 crisis. Ch2: too old for the war, or dies in it.
- **Sir Ancel Brome of Brome Tower.**
  - **Look and voice:** About forty-five, lean as a hayrake, half an ear gone. Says "Hm." Kind only by silence.
  - **What drives him:** Wants Thorne dead and the March quiet.
  - **History:** Owes Hugh Fletcher his life from Harrow Ford.

## Beyond Ravell
- **Sir Walter Pryce.**
  - **Look and voice:** Thirty-five, a younger son turned captain. Courteous and businesslike. "The Earl's company pays on time."
  - **What drives him:** Wants to be a banneret and then a lord. Recognises himself in rising men.
- **Dame Joan Wyck.**
  - **Look and voice:** Twenty-seven, practical, wry. Grieves by working.
  - **Situation:** Holds Wyck in her own right for now. Her stepson Ralph wants it.
- **Black Ewan of Glenhallow.**
  - **Look and voice:** A tall Caldmoor raider with a voice like a cart on stones. Laughs at everything except his family.

## Added in Chapter 3, Act III (the Match)
- **Aliénor de Brésy.** Twenty, Sire Gautier's daughter. Dark, straight-backed, watches everything. Speaks good Adalian and does not admit it at first. Remembers every bargain made over her head.
- **Sire Gautier de Brésy of Montbrun.** The Constable's younger brother, across the march. Heavy, tired, has buried two sons. Plain talk and good wine. Wants the border quiet more than he wants to win.
- **Lady Philippa Ashdown.** Nineteen, the King's ward, heiress of Ashdown (three manors in the south of Adalia). Small, fair, in black for her father and brothers, dead in the Mottle. Perfectly polite and furious at being given away.
- **Sir John Sayer.** Isabel's father, a poor shire knight with seven daughters. Ashamed of the size of every dowry.
- **Ralph Wyck.** Joan's stepson, twenty-one by Ch3, with a lawyer and a grievance.
- **Sire Aimery de Vaux.** Clémence's brother-in-law in the south. Blames the Adalians for his brother's death.
- **Piero Lanzi.** Fiammetta's cousin in Sarenza, the house's choice for her. "Has never once in his life surprised anybody."
- **Maestro Orsini.** Sarenzan physician in Sauvemer. Horoscopes, bleeding, great confidence.
